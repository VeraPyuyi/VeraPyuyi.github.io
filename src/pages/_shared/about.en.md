## Hi, I’m Pyuyi

Mathematics has a kind of proof that says only ‘there is’, never ‘where’.

Like the warmth left on a chair; like half a line of working not quite wiped from the blackboard; like rings spreading on the water after the fish has gone.

Every page here is evidence of that kind: a paper, a few lines of code, a photograph taken against the light, half a sentence left unfinished. They all point to the same person, and each stops just short.

The little distance that remains, I am still walking myself.

> The black hole at the centre of the Milky Way was first recognised the same way: no one saw it, only a few stars circling a patch of apparent emptiness, year after year.
