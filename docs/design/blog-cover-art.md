# Blog cover art

The first five bilingual blog covers were generated on 2026-08-30 with the built-in GPT Image tool. The site's original
`public/og.png` illustration was supplied as a **style reference only** for the star-cat identity and the broad pastel
palette; its composition was not edited or reproduced. Every final cover is a newly generated Japanese 2D anime
scene, contains no third-party character, and is shared by its Chinese and English article pair.

## Recurring original researcher

The same original adult researcher appears throughout the cover collection:

- a woman clearly in her twenties with normal adult anime proportions;
- a slender natural build and small bust, without exaggerated curves or sexualized poses;
- waist-length hair transitioning from sapphire blue through lavender to cherry pink;
- blue-violet eyes and one small gold star hairpin;
- a cream high-collar blouse, short powder-blue cardigan, deep navy knee-length pleated skirt, opaque navy knee
  socks, and simple shoes;
- a curious, capable, gentle expression, accompanied by the original dark navy star cat.

The Monte Carlo cover established the character anchor. Each later prompt used that selected image as the identity,
body, outfit, and anime-rendering reference. The recurring character must not be shortened, recolored, made chibi, or
redesigned between covers.

## Shared final prompt

Use case: `stylized-concept`. Asset type: original 16:9 Japanese anime blog-header key visual. Preserve the recurring
researcher's exact face, long gradient hair, modest outfit, slender small-bust build, and normal adult proportions.
Use clean expressive line art, polished cel shading, detailed hair, layered research-room backgrounds, crisp
highlights, and gentle pastel color scripting. Keep the research concept as a major readable subject rather than a
background ornament. Place essential content inside the central 70% for mobile cropping and leave a calmer, slightly
darker title region for white webpage text.

Every cover must be entirely original and contain no text, letters, numbers, equations, code, labels, logos,
trademarks, watermarks, insignia, sexualized pose, duplicated character, or copyrighted character. Avoid chibi or
sticker art, photorealism, 3D rendering, and the painterly cosmic-landscape composition used by the paper series.

## Processing and use

The selected outputs were center-normalized to 1600×900 PNG files with metadata removed. The regular image build
creates local 480, 960, and 1600 pixel AVIF/WebP variants plus an LQIP. These illustrations belong to the content
layer; the site's AGPL source-code license does not imply that unrelated third-party character art is included.
